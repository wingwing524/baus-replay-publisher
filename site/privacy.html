Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026. Baus Replay Publisher is operated by Isaac Yam for the owner-controlled Baus Replay — Fan Edits channel.

Google and YouTube data

With the owner’s consent, the local tool accesses channel identity, video metadata, publication status and public engagement statistics. Upload authorization allows the owner to upload video files and thumbnails. Additional publishing or analytics permissions, if separately granted on Google’s consent screen, are used only to manage the owner’s channel or review its performance.

Use and storage

Data is used to publish the owner’s edits, prevent duplicate uploads, verify publication and review channel performance. OAuth credentials are kept in a private credential directory outside public project/review folders. Video IDs, titles, source credits, status records and performance snapshots may be retained in the owner’s project records. Selected public metadata and aggregate performance evidence may be included in public project documentation; authorization tokens and private account credentials are never intentionally published.

Processing and disclosure

The tool runs on the owner’s computers. Google processes API requests and hosts uploaded content under its own policies. The owner may use an AI-assisted workspace to operate the tool and inspect relevant project records. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or credit decisions, or used to train a generalized AI model. People do not access the data except as authorized by the owner, as necessary for security or legal compliance, or as otherwise permitted by Google’s Limited Use requirements.

Baus Replay Publisher’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Credentials are retained while the owner wants the connection active. The owner can revoke access in Google Account connections and remove local credentials and retained records. Revocation prevents further authorized API access; it does not delete already published videos. Deletion of published videos is a separate channel action.

This website

This static site has no login form, advertising, tracking scripts or application cookies. GitHub Pages serves the site and may process ordinary request information under its privacy statement.
