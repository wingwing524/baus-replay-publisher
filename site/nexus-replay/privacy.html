Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026.

Nexus Replay Publisher is an owner-operated local desktop application. It requests YouTube upload permission and read-only access to YouTube channel and video information to verify the destination and operate the owner's video workflow.

Google authorization credentials, refresh tokens and local production records are stored on the owner's computer. The operator does not sell this information or share it with advertising or analytics providers. Prepared media and metadata are sent to YouTube when the owner uses the upload function.

The operator can remove locally stored credentials and records and revoke access through Google Account permissions. Removing local credentials does not delete previously uploaded YouTube videos; those remain under the owner's YouTube account controls.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This public information site is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting requests are subject to GitHub's privacy statement. The site includes no operator-added tracking scripts or sign-in forms.
