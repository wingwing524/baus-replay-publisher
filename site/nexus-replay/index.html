Nexus Replay Publisher

A local desktop tool operated by Isaac Yam for the Nexus Replay esports highlights channel.

The publisher uploads prepared videos and thumbnails and reads the owner's channel identity and video information through the YouTube Data API. Authorization is limited to the owner-selected channel. Captions are managed in YouTube Studio.

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on the owner's computer. This website provides public app information; it does not collect Google sign-ins or host the publisher.
